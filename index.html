2026–27 Registration Fees

A full breakdown of what's included in your fees, how payments are structured, and the schedule for the 2026–27 season.

What's included in your fees, how payments work, and what to expect across the 2026-27 season.

For returning families: the Board reviewed program and coaching costs this season while fighting increasing costs on all fronts. Fees were not automatically indexed, and increases from our facility and governing bodies were not passed on. Across all groups, fees have decreased by 2.3% compared to last season.

Understanding Your Fees

Your season fees have three parts. Here is what each one covers.

Registration Fee
A one-time annual fee covering club membership and registration with Swim Ontario and Swimming Canada, the provincial and national governing bodies for competitive swimming. $425 for competitive members; $200 for recreational members. For ROW Swim Academy, the registration fee is included in the training fee.
Training Fees
Covers coaching and pool rental for the season. The amount reflects the level and cost of coaching for your group. Club administration and coach mentoring costs are shared equally across all swimmers.
Prepaid Meet Fees
Meet entry costs for the season, spread equally across your regular monthly payments. Rather than billing each meet separately as it comes up, the expected cost for the year is built into your payment plan. The amount has been set this season to accurately reflect a typical year for each group. REC and ROW Swim Academy groups have no prepaid meet fees.
Total Due
Registration Fee plus Training Fees plus Prepaid Meet Fees. Divided across an initial September payment and monthly instalments.

Payment Schedule

Fees are collected in two stages: an initial payment in September 2026, followed by equal monthly payments starting October 15.

September 2026
First payment, equal to the Registration Fee: $425 for competitive members; $200 for recreational members. ROW Swim Academy pays in full at this step.
October 15 onwards
Monthly payments. The remaining balance splits into 10 instalments for competitive groups (National Development through Junior Development 2), and 9 for TOPS and REC. See the table below for your group's monthly amount.
Monthly payments are a billing schedule, not equal monthly programming. All groups train September through June, with some running into July for provincial or national competitions. The payment structure reflects how fees are collected, not the volume of programming in any given month.

Payment Methods

Choose the payment method that works best for your family.

1
Full in advance
Pay the full season balance at registration. No further payments required.
2
Monthly by Automatic Funds Transfer
Your bank account is debited automatically on the 15th of each month.
3
Monthly by Member Initiated e-Transfer
You send an e-Transfer on or before the 15th of each month.
4
Monthly Automatic Credit Card payment*
Your credit card is charged automatically on the 15th of each month.
* A processing fee applies to automatic credit card payments.

One payment method must be committed to at the start of the season. To change your method during the season, contact the ROW Club Office at office@rowswimming.ca.

What Gets Billed When

Not everything is collected upfront. Here is how billing works across the year.

Registration & Training Fees
Billed at the start of the season. ROW Swim Academy fees are billed at the start of each session.
Swim Meet Fees
Expected meet costs are built into your monthly payment plan. Any entries above that estimate are billed when they occur. REC groups have no prepaid amount; meet costs are billed at the time of each meet.
Special Events
Required in advance for club events such as the annual awards banquet and bowling nights.
Team Travel Meets
Handled separately; not included in prepaid meet fees.
Your account balance is available at any time through your online account on the website.

Groups at ROW

A quick guide to the group types.

Competitive Groups
National Development, Provincial Development, Senior Development, Age Group Development, and Junior Development groups train and compete in sanctioned meets. Placement reflects your swimmer's age, ability, and goals.
TOPS
An entry point into competitive swimming, with two or three sessions per week. TOPS swimmers enter sanctioned competitions and carry full registration with Swim Ontario and Swimming Canada.
REC
Recreational swimming, available in morning (AM) and afternoon/evening (PM) sessions. No competition requirements.
ROW Swim Academy
An introductory program for swimmers new to the sport. Runs in sessions rather than the full year; fees billed at the start of each session. Registration is included in the training fee. No meet fees.
Not sure which group applies? Placement is confirmed by coaching staff when you register. Contact office@rowswimming.ca with questions before registering.
